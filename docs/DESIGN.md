## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-conversion, modern automotive marketplace environment centered around uncompromising transparency, certified quality, and frictionless digital purchasing. Built for modern car buyers navigating used car inventories, the interface blends authoritative corporate reliability with lively consumer digital-retail clarity.

The visual style unites **Modern Corporate** reliability with clean **Glassmorphic and Tonal Layering**:
- **Trust-Driven Rigor:** Crisp contrast lines, structured specification tables, and persistent verification indicators remove purchase anxiety.
- **Conversion-Oriented Energy:** Strategic vibrant sky blues and assurance greens draw visual attention to action drivers (e.g., booking test drives, viewing 200-point inspection reports, EMI calculation CTAs).
- **Tactile Utility:** Micro-elevations, structured chips, and responsive listing cards that mimic modern fintech speed and consumer transparency.

## Colors

The palette balances authoritative deep corporate navies with dynamic modern blues and validation greens directly derived from institutional trust standards and vehicle certification cues:

- **Primary (`#0B2545` & `#091E3A`):** Anchors headers, primary navigation, prominent car titles, and major transactional commands, conveying institutional strength and longevity.
- **Secondary (`#0284C7` & `#0EA5E9`):** Acts as the interactive vehicle for filter selections, interactive loans/EMI sliders, dynamic photo carousel controls, and secondary engagement points.
- **Tertiary (`#16A34A` & `#22C55E`):** Reserved exclusively for trust validation—200-point inspection checkmarks, certified warranty badges, "Great Price" indicators, and instant deposit confirmation buttons.
- **Neutrals (`#64748B`, `#F8FAFC`, `#FFFFFF`):** Form the baseline for high-legibility specs (mileage, transmission, fuel type, owner count) across light, airy backgrounds that eliminate catalog fatigue.

## Typography

Typography relies on **Plus Jakarta Sans** across all hierarchies to combine modern geometric tech aesthetics with approachable curves. Numeric values—such as vehicle pricing, odometer readouts, and estimated monthly payments—gain instant legibility through semi-bold and bold weights.

- **Headlines:** Use heavy weights (`700` and `800`) with tighter negative letter-spacing for vehicle listings and value propositions, creating immediate visual impact.
- **Labels & Badges:** Utilize uppercase styling with slight positive letter spacing (`0.02em` to `0.04em`) on mini tags (`label-sm`), such as "VERIFIED INSPECTION", "ASSURED", and "ZERO DOWN PAYMENT".
- **Body & Specs:** Maintained at regular and medium weights (`400` and `500`) to guarantee effortless scanning across multi-parameter grid lists (e.g., fuel type, transmission, model year, and mileage).

## Layout & Spacing

The design system is constructed on a fluid, responsive 12-column grid layout on desktop and tablet, collapsing down to a 4-column flow on mobile viewports.

- **Breakpoints:**
  - **Desktop (>= 1200px):** 12 columns, 24px gutters, max-width container 1280px. Allows a 3-column or 4-column car inventory card grid alongside a sticky faceted filter sidebar.
  - **Tablet (768px – 1199px):** 8 columns, 20px gutters, sticky top or horizontal chip filter bar, 2-column car inventory cards.
  - **Mobile (< 768px):** 4 columns, 16px gutters, full-bleed horizontal scroll car carousels, and single-column vehicle cards with bottom-sheet filter modals.

- **Spacing Rhythm:** Standardized multiples of 4px and 8px govern whitespace. Internal vehicle card padding sits strictly at `space-md` (16px), while car listing grids maintain `space-lg` (24px) spacing to preserve distinct silhouette boundaries between vehicle photographs.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy adopts subtle ambient shadows combined with clean, low-contrast structural borders:

- **Level 0 (Flat / Canvas):** Applied to the base canvas background (`#F8FAFC`).
- **Level 1 (Listing Cards & Filters):** Applied to resting inventory cards and filter panels. Consists of a 1px boundary border (`#E2E8F0`) paired with an ambient shadow: `0 1px 3px 0 rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Hover & Active Surfaces):** Elevated state when hovering over a vehicle card or active search bar: `0 10px 25px -5px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.08), 0 8px 10px -6px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.04)`, shifting the border stroke to a soft secondary tint (`#BAE6FD`).
- **Level 3 (Sticky CTAs & Modals):** Sticky booking banners, inspection report popovers, and 360-view modals utilize: `0 20px 25px -5px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.12), 0 10px 10px -5px rgba(11, 37, 69, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The design uses a balanced rounded approach (`roundedness: 2`) that brings approachable friendliness while retaining crisp, professional automotive alignment:

- **Standard Containers & Cards (`rounded-lg`, 16px):** Used for vehicle listing cards, financing calculators, and verification feature highlights.
- **Interactive Controls (`rounded`, 8px):** Applied to primary action buttons, text input fields, drop-down menus, and modal dialogs.
- **Pills & Status Badges (Full Rounding / 9999px):** Applied to vehicle attributes (e.g., "Petrol", "Automatic", "1st Owner"), inspection score tags ("200-Point Inspected"), and promotional chips ("Free Test Drive").

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary CTA:** Solid navy background (`#0B2545`), white text, bold weight, 8px border-radius, medium shadow. Hover transitions to `#091E3A` with subtle scale lift.
- **Secondary Action (Booking / Direct Action):** Solid vibrant blue (`#0284C7`), white text; used for direct conversions like "Book Free Test Drive".
- **Trust / Verification Action:** Vibrant Trust Green (`#16A34A`), white text; reserved for "Reserve Now" or "Buy Online".
- **Outlined / Ghost:** Transparent background with 1.5px stroke of slate neutral (`#CBD5E1`) and navy typography.

### Vehicle Inventory Cards
- **Image Header:** 16:10 aspect ratio vehicle photograph with absolute-positioned trust badges at top-left ("Value Certified" with green checkmark icon) and a floating favorite/heart icon at top-right.
- **Meta Row:** Key details (Year, Brand, Model, Variant) in `headline-sm` (`#0B2545`).
- **Feature Specs Row:** Horizontal inline list or flex chips displaying Odometer reading (e.g., "34,200 km"), Fuel Type ("Petrol"), Transmission ("Manual"), and RTO location with slate dividers.
- **Price & EMI Footer:** Two-tier presentation featuring total price in large bold typography alongside calculated monthly installment (e.g., "EMI from $280/mo") and zero-down indicators.

### Chips & Specification Tags
- Built with 9999px pill shapes, 6px vertical and 12px horizontal padding, subtle slate backgrounds (`#F1F5F9`), and dark slate typography (`#334155`).
- Inspection and Guarantee chips feature tinted light green backgrounds (`#DCFCE7`) paired with deep green text (`#166534`) and a prominent check icon.

### Form Inputs & Filters
- Clean inputs with 1px border stroke (`#CBD5E1`), transitioning to secondary electric blue border (`#0284C7`) on focus with an ambient ring focus glow (`rgba(2, 132, 199, 0.15)`).
- Budget range sliders use dual-point continuous sliders styled with electric blue filled rails and crisp white rounded thumbs.

### Checkboxes & Radio Selectors
- Checkbox borders use 1.5px `#94A3B8`. When checked, the fill transitions to `#0B2545` with a crisp white tick mark.
- Vehicle feature checklist in the inspection report uses solid `#16A34A` circular checks to indicate passed checkpoints.
