# Using your website — a simple guide

This is your guide to adding cars, talking to customers, and updating your website. You don't need to know anything technical. If you can use WhatsApp, you can use this.

Everything here happens on your **admin panel**, which is a private part of the website only you and your staff can see. Customers never see it.

---

## 1. Logging in

1. Go to `yourdomain.in/admin/login` (bookmark this on your phone).
2. Type your email and password.
3. Tap **Sign in**.

If you forget your password, ask whoever set up the website to reset it for you — there's no "forgot password" link yet.

---

## 2. Adding a car (works great from your phone)

1. After logging in, tap **Add Car** in the menu.
2. Fill in the details, step by step:
   - **Brand and Model** — start typing (e.g. "Hyun...") and pick it from the list. If a brand or model isn't listed, type its full name and tap **Add**.
   - **Variant** — the trim name written on the car's papers, e.g. "SX Petrol AT".
   - **Selling price** — just the numbers, e.g. `750000` for ₹7,50,000. It shows you how that will look on the website as you type.
   - **Year, kilometres driven, fuel, transmission, body type, owners** — pick each one.
   - **Colour, registration state/city** — optional but nice to fill in.
   - **Features** — tick everything the car has (sunroof, reverse camera, and so on).
   - **Description** — a few honest sentences: condition, service history, anything a buyer should know.
3. Scroll to **Photos** and add your photos (see the photo tips below).
4. When you're done, scroll to the bottom:
   - Tap **Save draft** if you're not ready to show it on the website yet. You can come back and finish it later — it stays private until you publish it.
   - Tap **Publish** to put it live on the website straight away. You need at least one photo to publish.

That's it — the car appears on the website within seconds, no waiting.

### Taking good photos

You don't need a professional camera — your phone is enough.

- **Take these photos**: front, rear, both sides, the dashboard, the front seats, the boot, the tyres, and the engine bay.
- **10–20 photos** is a good number. More than that is rarely necessary.
- Clean the car and park it somewhere with good light and a tidy background before you start.
- Take photos in landscape (sideways), not portrait — the website shows them wider than they are tall.
- On the **Photos** step, tap **Choose photos** (or **Take photo** to use your camera right there) — you can pick several at once. Wait for each one to finish uploading (you'll see a progress bar) before you leave the page.
- **Drag a photo to reorder it.** The first photo is the one customers see first everywhere on the website — pick your best one and drag it to the front. Look for the **Make cover** button on any photo to move it to the front instead.
- If a photo fails to upload (bad signal, for example), you'll see a **Retry** button on it.

## 3. Changing a car's status

Open **Cars** in the menu to see everything you've listed. Each car has a status:

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Draft** | Only you can see it. Not on the website yet. |
| **Published** | Live on the website, customers can see and enquire about it. |
| **Reserved** | Still shown on the website, but with an orange "Reserved" label, so people know a deal is in progress. |
| **Sold** | Removed from the website. Its old link shows customers similar cars instead of an error. |
| **Archived** | Hidden everywhere — use this for old listings you want to keep a record of but never show again. |

**To change a car's status:**

1. Find the car in **Cars**, or open it by tapping **Edit**.
2. Tap the **⋯ (more actions)** button, or **Status** at the bottom of the edit page.
3. Pick the new status, e.g. **Mark reserved** or **Mark sold**.
4. Confirm if asked. It updates on the website immediately.

If a deal falls through after you've marked a car reserved or sold, you can always change it back to **Mark available** — nothing is lost.

**A note on deleting:** once a car has been published, you can't permanently delete it — this is on purpose, so you always have a record of what you've sold. Use **Archived** instead. Only a car still in **Draft** (never published) can be deleted for good, from the **⋯** menu.

## 4. Handling enquiries (leads)

When someone fills in a form on your website (from a car's page or the Contact page), it shows up under **Leads** in your menu, along with a red counter so you know something new has come in.

1. Tap **Leads**.
2. Each enquiry shows the person's name, phone number, what car (if any) they asked about, and their message.
3. Tap the **call** or **WhatsApp** icon to get in touch straight away — it opens your phone's dialler or WhatsApp with their number ready.
4. As you work the enquiry, update its status using the dropdown: **New → Contacted → Test drive → Negotiation → Closed** (or **Lost** if it didn't work out). This is just for you to keep track — customers never see it.
5. You can add private notes to a lead (e.g. "Said he'll call back Monday") — only staff see these.
6. Use the search box at the top to find a lead by name or phone number, or to see every enquiry about one particular car.

If you've asked for email alerts to be turned on, you'll also get an email the moment a new enquiry comes in.

## 5. Changing your homepage text and photos

Tap **Homepage Content** in the menu. This controls the words and pictures on your homepage and About page — no coding, just fill in boxes:

- **Hero** — the big headline and photo (or short video) at the very top of your homepage, plus the button under it (e.g. "Browse Cars").
- **Why choose us** — up to a few short reasons customers should buy from you (e.g. "Transparent pricing"). Edit the wording or add/remove one.
- **Dealership video** — paste a YouTube link here if you have a video tour of your showroom; it shows on the homepage.
- **About page** — the text on your About page.

Which cars show as "Featured" on the homepage is decided from each car's own page — open a car in **Cars**, and there's a **Featured car** switch near the bottom.

Tap **Save** at the bottom of whichever section you changed. Changes appear on the live website right away — open your website in another tab to check.

---

## 6. Your contact details

Tap **Settings** to update:

- Your dealership's name and logo.
- Phone number, WhatsApp number, address, Google Maps link, and business hours.
- Links to your social media pages (optional).

These appear in your website's header, footer and Contact page automatically, everywhere at once — you only need to change them in this one place.

---

## 7. Customer reviews (testimonials)

Tap **Testimonials** to add a review: the customer's name, a star rating, their words, and an optional photo. There's a **Show on website** switch — turn it on when you're ready for a review to go live, and off to hide one without deleting it.

---

## 8. If something goes wrong

- **A photo won't upload**: check your internet connection and tap **Retry**. If it keeps failing, try a smaller or different photo.
- **You can't log in**: double-check your email and password. If you still can't get in, contact whoever set up the website — don't try creating a new account yourself, only they can do that safely.
- **A car isn't showing on the website**: check its status in **Cars** — it needs to be **Published** (or **Reserved**) and needs at least one photo.
- **Anything else looks broken**: contact whoever built or maintains your website with a screenshot and a short description of what you were doing. That's always faster than trying to guess the fix yourself.
